### Printing problems

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
| ------- | ----- | --- |
| The slicer complains about floating or disconnected parts | You exported with a red warning tag showing; the tag is a separate object | Fix the named measurement first |{split_row}
| Layers split at the pocket floor or around a hole | Under-extrusion, or too few walls | 3 to 4 walls and 25 percent infill or more |
| The tool snapped while pulling | A brittle material, or walls too thin | {strength_fix} |
