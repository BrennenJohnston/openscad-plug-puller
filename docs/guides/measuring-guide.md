# Measuring Guide

Everything is measured in mm. Pick your tool first: a plug up to 24 mm thick takes the one-sided puller; thicker, or a USB-C tip or a round extension-cord plug, the two-sided puller.

| Tool | The measuring guide | The form |
| ---- | ------------------- | -------- |
| One-sided puller, `src/Plug_Puller_Parametric.scad` | [Measuring guide, one-sided puller](one-sided/measuring-guide.md) | [Measuring form, one-sided puller](one-sided/measuring-form.md); the sheet to print: [measuring-form.svg](one-sided/measuring-form.svg) |
| Two-sided puller, `src/Plug_Puller_Two_Sided.scad` | [Measuring guide, two-sided puller](two-sided/measuring-guide.md) | [Measuring form, two-sided puller](two-sided/measuring-form.md); the sheet to print: [measuring-form.svg](two-sided/measuring-form.svg) |

No caliper? The [printable measuring template](measuring-template.svg) has a mm ruler and finger circles, and the [measuring stencil](print-preview-outlines.md#the-measuring-stencil) is the same as printed cards.

Each tool's whole packet, with the quick start, every dial's before-and-after picture, the measuring guide and the form in one printable PDF: [`docs/Plug_Puller_One_Sided_Guide.pdf`](../Plug_Puller_One_Sided_Guide.pdf) and [`docs/Plug_Puller_Two_Sided_Guide.pdf`](../Plug_Puller_Two_Sided_Guide.pdf).
